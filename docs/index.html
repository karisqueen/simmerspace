New in 0.11.1 · CurseForge is built in

Find, install and organize your Sims 4 mods in one place.

SimmerSpace now comes with CurseForge built in: explore the Sims 4 catalog, install mods straight into your Mods folder and check for updates without leaving the app. It’s still a beta and some things are being polished, but you can already use it as your mod manager.

CurseForge, inside SimmerSpace

From finding a mod to playing with it, without leaving the app.

Sign in to your SimmerSpace account and the whole Sims 4 catalog on CurseForge is ready in Explore. There’s nothing else to set up.

Search the catalog

Browse Sims 4 mods and CC, sort by popular, recently updated or most downloaded, and open each mod’s description and files.

Install with one click

Each mod goes into its own “CurseForge - name” folder inside Mods. Your existing files are never replaced, and every download is checked before it’s installed.

Keep it up to date

The Installed tab lists everything you got through SimmerSpace and checks for new versions. Mods by creators you follow show up first.

When an author only allows downloads from the CurseForge website, SimmerSpace takes you there instead of installing.

Everything else your Mods folder needs

A cozy home for all your CC

My CC

Scan your Mods folder without changing anything, tag files by category and creator, enable or disable them, rename them and organize them into folders with a preview first.

Creators

Discover the most popular Sims 4 creators on CurseForge and follow your favorites.

Collections

Save mods into your own collections, each with its own plumbob color.

Inspiration

Share your builds and Sims with the community and find ideas from other players.

Make it yours

Eight themes in light and dark, compact mode, reduced motion, and the whole app in English or Spanish.

Ready to play

Launch The Sims 4 straight from SimmerSpace when your Mods are ready.

An honest beta

Ready to use, still being polished

The CurseForge integration and the manager already work, so you can use SimmerSpace day to day. Some details are still rough and new things arrive with each version. If something goes wrong, the Feedback button in Settings sends it straight to us.

Works today

  • Explore and install CurseForge mods
  • Update checks for the mods you installed
  • Scan, tag and organize your Mods folder
  • Creators, collections and the Inspiration feed
  • SimmerSpace accounts with an email code

Still to polish

  • Some screens and texts still need finishing touches
  • Patreon isn’t connected yet
  • The .exe isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows may show a warning
  • New versions are downloaded from this page for now
  • Windows only

As with any mod manager, back up your Mods folder before big changes.
