Made around your real Sims routine

A cozy home for your CC journey

Your game folders, your growing library, and all those little finds you want to come back to—SimmerSpace is bringing them together in one friendly desktop app.

⌂

Know where everything lives

Set your Sims 4 folders, keep the important paths close, and make your setup easier to manage.

▧

Make sense of your library

Scan your Mods folder and manage local files with tags, activation controls, renaming, and user-selected organization.

♡

Keep the good finds close

Explore the design direction for discovery, creators, inspiration boards, and collections.

The dream we're building toward

A proper mod manager, made with Simmers in mind.

The goal is a polished, installable desktop app that helps you find content, download and install it, and keep a large Mods folder organized and running smoothly. SimmerSpace is early, but that bigger picture is what guides every step.

A small, honest beginning

What this pre-beta can do today

Try the first pieces of the experience while SimmerSpace grows into the fuller manager it is meant to become.

✓

Game paths

Choose your Mods folder, Sims 4 user folder, game installation, and optional Sims 4 Studio location.

⌕

Local Mods management

Scan your Mods folder without changing anything, then tag, enable or disable, rename, or recycle files. New in 0.7.0: tidy files from nested folders into category folders, with a preview of every move, a choice to keep or flatten subfolders, duplicate names skipped or numbered (never overwritten), a Stop button and a per-file report.

✦

A feel for what's ahead

Browse the visual concepts for finding CC, following creators, saving inspiration, and sharing collections.

☁
Still in pre-beta

Online catalogs, creator integrations (Patreon, CurseForge and others), and in-app downloads or installation are not active. Any sample listings and creator profiles are fictional demonstration content. SimmerSpace runs on Windows, sends nothing over the internet, and the pre-beta build is not code-signed yet.
